About E-Shop

Your Trusted Online Shopping Platform

Who We Are

E-Shop is a modern three-tier e-commerce application developed using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript, Node.js, Express.js, MySQL, and AWS Cloud services.

Our platform enables customers to browse products, add items to the shopping cart, place orders securely, and track their purchases while administrators manage products, users, and orders from a dedicated dashboard.

Our Mission

To provide customers with a secure, reliable and user-friendly online shopping experience while leveraging AWS Cloud technologies for scalability and high availability.

Our Vision

To build a cloud-powered e-commerce platform that delivers excellent customer satisfaction through innovation, security and performance.
